01 — THE PROJECT

More than a product.
An experience.

Pixel Pulse is a design thesis focused on Advertising in Digital Media for Electronic Gaming Headsets. The research explores gamer preferences, important headset features, digital platforms and existing advertising approaches.

The project uses the research findings to develop a cinematic advertising direction that presents a gaming headset through experience and storytelling, not only technical specifications.

02 — CONCEPT & IDEATION

Show the feeling
of gaming.

◉

The concept

A cinematic digital advertisement that transforms an ordinary gaming session into an immersive visual and audio experience.

✦

Visual language

Futuristic neon environments, purple and blue lighting, sound-wave graphics, digital particles and dramatic product close-ups.

⌁

Story direction

A gamer discovers a more immersive experience through the headset, moving from distraction to focus and connection with the game.

03 — RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Understand the
gaming audience.

The objective is to study the role of digital media in advertising electronic gaming headsets by understanding gamer preferences, identifying important product features, and analysing existing advertising strategies.

01 Understand gamer preferences and behaviour.
02 Identify the headset features gamers value.
03 Explore platforms where gaming advertisements appear.
04 Analyse visual styles and advertising content.
05 Develop a visual direction for the final advertisement.
